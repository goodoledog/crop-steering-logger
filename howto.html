Crop Steering Logger, how to use it

You walk the flower room with a scale and a phone. The app tells you what to run and writes every number to the Sheet. Zones are Z1 to Z6. Each zone has 4 lights, L1 to L4. The sentinel bag under each light is named Z3-L2 style.

First time on a phone

  1. Open the app link in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone).
  2. Add it to the home screen: Chrome menu, Add to Home screen. Safari: share button, Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Me at the bottom, type your name, tap Save name. Do this each time you open the app.

Every checkpoint, every bag

  1. Tap Log. Tap the zone.
  2. Tap the bag (the light it sits under). The strain shows under the button.
  3. Tap the checkpoint: 9:25 AM, 11:15 AM, 4:15 PM, or Lights-off.
  4. Put the whole bag on the scale. Type the weight in grams. At 11:15 also type leachate mL, and EC and pH if you measured them.
  5. Tap Save and get decision. Wait for the card. Green is on plan. Orange means do what the card says.
  6. Tap Next bag and repeat. Tap New zone when the zone is done.

A red card means it did not save. Your numbers stay in the boxes. Get signal and tap Save again. Never move on from a red card.

What the card tells you

CheckpointWhat you get
9:25 AMP1 shot count for this bag and for the zone (mean of the zone's bags logged so far). The TrolMaster line is what you program: start, run time, interval, events, last start. Log all 4 bags, then use the zone line from the last card.
11:15 AMThis weight becomes the bag's new field capacity, if it is sane. Tomorrow's math uses it. Leachate warnings show here.
4:15 PMP2 YES or NO. YES means add a P2 shot at 3:30 PM tomorrow.
Lights-offOn target, or move the last shot to 4:30 PM tomorrow.

When you did not run what the app said

Tap Deviation. Pick the zone, the type (skipped shot, moved start, changed count, other), what was planned, what you ran, and why. The reason is required. Save.

Harvest

Tap Harvest. Pick zone and light, type wet and dry grams, save. The strain fills itself from the Sheet.

Today

Tap Today for one card per zone: the flower day, the last 9:25 decision, and everything logged so far.

Where the data lives

Google Sheet Crop Steering Master, tabs that start with CS_. CS_Settings holds every number the rules use. CS_Bags holds each bag's field capacity and strain. Do not edit the other tabs.

Room setup (flip dates and strains)

Tap Me, scroll to Room setup. Each zone has its flip date (day 1 of flower) and four strain boxes, L1 to L4. Tap Save room setup. Do this before day 1 of a crop, or any time a strain changes.
